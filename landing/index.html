Frequently asked questions

What is CrewCoach?
CrewCoach is a web application built for dragon boat teams. It lets coaches manage paddler rosters, build race-specific crewlists, and generate optimized seat configurations — all from one place. No spreadsheets, no guesswork.
Do I need an account?
Yes — a free account keeps your data secure and synced across devices. Sign up takes less than a minute with your email address.
What data do I need for each paddler?
At minimum, a name and weight. For best results, add erg score, preferred paddling side, seat preference (front / middle / back), gender, and role (paddler, drummer, sweep). The more data you provide, the smarter the auto-config becomes.
How does auto lineup work?
CrewCoach runs a greedy optimization algorithm that tries three different orderings — by weight, by power, and a combined score — then picks the lineup with the best overall balance. It accounts for left/right weight distribution, power ratios, and individual side and bench preferences.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. All data is stored securely in Firebase under your personal account. No one else can access your rosters, crewlists, or configurations unless you share them.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. CrewCoach is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The drag-and-drop editor supports touch input, so you can adjust lineups right at the dock.
Can I manage multiple boats or events?
Yes. Create as many crewlists and configurations as you need. Each crewlist can have its own age division, boat size, and category — perfect for festivals with multiple race categories.
